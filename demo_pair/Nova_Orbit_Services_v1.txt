SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Nova Tech Pvt Ltd (the "Supplier") and Orbit Retail Ltd (the "Client").

1. Services
The Supplier shall provide the software services described in Schedule A to the Client.

2. Fees and Payment
The Client shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule A within 30 days of receipt of each invoice.

3. Late Payment
If the Client fails to pay any invoice under Clause 2, interest of 2% per month shall accrue on the overdue amount.

4. Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of 12 months.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 60 days written notice. Clause 3 shall survive termination.

6. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for five years after termination.

7. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client under Clause 2 in the preceding twelve months.

8. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against third party claims that the Services infringe intellectual property rights.

9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts of Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

10. Notices
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set out in Schedule A.
